FIXED COMPOSITION 06

One closed smartphone stands vertically on its bottom edge, seen at an extreme side-biased three-quarter angle. Approximate bounds x=40–64%, y=23–79%; its apparent center lies slightly right of the frame center. The left near edge is almost vertical, with a rounded top corner around (42%,23%). Camera roll is zero and camera height is close to the phone midpoint.
SCENE: smooth cool blue-gray seamless background, soft luminous floor band near y≈64%. Pale satin floor with a controlled warm-white patch at the base. Key light comes from upper right, creating a small bright glint near the top rear corner and a long soft dark shadow extending toward the lower left. Keep the unusually large empty margins on both sides and above. No pedestal, no floating.

MATCHING RULES
Image A is the visual authority. The percentages above are approximate guides to observed framing, not measured camera calibration. Follow A's actual corner positions, silhouette orientation, negative space, camera azimuth/elevation, perspective compression and floor light pattern. Never mirror or reverse the composition. Preserve B's native proportions; match relative height and placement without stretching, cutting off new camera modules or distorting the hardware. Re-light B consistently with the fixed scene. Background must remain the same neutral color family and brightness pattern even when B has a different material/color. Do not adapt the background palette to the new phone.
If B does not show an essential side or rear detail required by this view, request another product photo rather than inventing hardware or artwork.

RENDERING
Photoreal commercial product photograph. Accurate hard-surface geometry, fine material texture, readable true engravings, controlled specular highlights, neutral white balance and realistic contact physics. No added graphic titles, captions, watermarks or logos. Keep the original reference scene clean and empty.
