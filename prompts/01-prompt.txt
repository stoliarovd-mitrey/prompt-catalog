FIXED COMPOSITION 01
Output aspect ratio 1143:2048; vertical 9:16-like portrait canvas, ideally 1143 × 2048.
A single closed smartphone stands upright on its bottom edge, rear panel facing the camera almost perfectly square-on. The vertical axis is upright without roll. Only a very narrow sliver of its side/second folded layer is visible at the right edge; do not turn this into a strong three-quarter view. Match the flat top and bottom edge directions and the reference silhouette. Entire phone is visible. Approximate object bounds on the full canvas: x=18–82%, y=23–76%, with the contact point at y≈75.5%. Its visual center sits below the canvas center, with large uninterrupted headroom. Preserve the new phone's own aspect ratio, using the same height occupancy rather than stretching it to the original width.
SCENE: seamless cool light silver-gray studio cyclorama, slightly darker toward the upper part, brightening to a nearly white horizontal floor region near y≈68–71%, without a hard drawn horizon. Smooth pale semi-gloss floor. Broad soft directional illumination from upper right/front produces a wide low-contrast cast shadow extending leftward along the floor. Keep the soft, vertically stretched, heavily defocused reflection below the base, fading toward the bottom edge; do not make a crisp mirror copy. No pedestal or props.

MATCHING RULES
Image A is the visual authority. The percentages above are approximate guides to observed framing, not measured camera calibration. Follow A's actual corner positions, silhouette orientation, negative space, camera azimuth/elevation, perspective compression and floor light pattern. Never mirror or reverse the composition. Preserve B's native proportions; match relative height and placement without stretching, cutting off new camera modules or distorting the hardware. Re-light B consistently with the fixed scene. Background must remain the same neutral color family and brightness pattern even when B has a different material/color. Do not adapt the background palette to the new phone.
If B does not show an essential side or rear detail required by this view, request another product photo rather than inventing hardware or artwork.

RENDERING
Photoreal commercial product photograph. Accurate hard-surface geometry, fine material texture, readable true engravings, controlled specular highlights, neutral white balance and realistic contact physics. No added graphic titles, captions, watermarks or logos. Keep the original reference scene clean and empty.
